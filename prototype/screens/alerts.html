Fake Maybank Login Pages Spreading on WhatsApp
2 days ago warning 237 reports in Selangor
Scammers are distributing fake Maybank2u login pages via WhatsApp. These pages look identical to the real thing but steal your credentials. Always type maybank2u.com.my directly into your browser.

Fake "LHDN Tax Refund" SMS Targeting KL Residents
3 days ago warning 182 reports in KL
SMS messages claiming you have a tax refund from LHDN are fake. They link to phishing pages that steal your banking info. LHDN never sends refund links via SMS.

Fake Shopee "Lucky Draw" Links on Facebook
5 days ago warning 94 reports in Penang
Ads on Facebook promising Shopee lucky draw prizes are scams. They lead to fake sites that collect your personal data. Shopee only runs promotions within their official app.

Malicious APK Disguised as "TNG eWallet Update"
1 week ago warning 156 reports in Johor
A fake APK file is being shared on Telegram claiming to be a TNG eWallet update. It installs malware that steals your OTP codes. Only update apps through Google Play Store.
