Anti-Scam Tips

Scammers send APK files through messaging apps disguised as legitimate apps, updates, or tools. Never download and install APK files received through these channels.

The Google Play Store screens apps for malware and malicious behavior. Installing apps from other sources bypasses these safety checks.

Google Play Protect continuously scans your device for potentially harmful apps. It should always be enabled for maximum protection.

Legitimate companies never ask you to sideload an APK. If someone sends you an APK file and asks you to install it, treat it as a scam.
