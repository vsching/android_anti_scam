How Scams Actually Work

It only takes 3 steps for scammers to steal everything.

1

Scammer sends an APK via WhatsApp

Disguised as a "Maybank update", "LHDN tax refund", or "Shopee lucky draw" — the link looks real but leads to a malicious download.

2

Your phone installs it

The app asks for accessibility permissions, then silently reads your SMS, steals your TAC codes, and takes over your banking apps.

3

Money gone. Savings wiped.

Within minutes, the scammer transfers everything out. Victims lose RM 10,000 to RM 500,000 — some lose their entire life savings.
